# Kinetix privacy policy

_Last updated 12 October 2026_

## The short version

Kinetix works entirely on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you create an account (to back up and sync) or connect Strava. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising and never track you across other apps or websites.

## What's stored on your iPhone

Your answers from setup (experience, goal, training days, equipment, units), your plan, the runs and lifts you log (including routes, splits, heart rate and effort), personal records, recovery check-ins and your settings.

## If you create an account

Your email address (or the private relay address from Sign in with Apple) and a copy of the data above are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so your training is backed up and syncs to your other devices. Only you can read your records.

## Location

Used only while you're recording a run, to measure distance and pace and draw your route. The route is saved with that run.

## Apple Health

Only if you connect it. Kinetix reads heart rate, resting heart rate and workouts from other apps to show live heart rate and balance your training load, and saves your runs and strength sessions to Health. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone for marketing. Heart rate recorded during a Kinetix run is kept with that run (and backed up to your account if you have one).

## Bluetooth

Used only to connect the heart rate strap you choose. The strap's identifier is stored on this iPhone so it can reconnect.

## Strava

Only if you connect it. Kinetix uploads the sessions you've chosen (runs, and strength sessions if you turn that on) to your Strava account. The Strava sign-in is kept in this iPhone's secure Keychain. Disconnecting revokes Kinetix's access. What happens on Strava is covered by Strava's own privacy policy.

## Your choices

Export everything as a file in Settings → Account & data. Delete your account and all your data, on this iPhone and in the cloud, from the same place. Disconnect Apple Health in the Health app and Strava in Settings → Integrations at any time.

## Children

Kinetix isn't intended for children under 16.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and on our website with a new date. For questions, use the support link on Kinetix's App Store page.

# Seeing the app without a Mac

Every push builds the app on GitHub's Macs and runs an automated walk-through that
taps through the screens like a person would.

1. Open https://github.com/georgeodwyer/New-Fitness-App/actions
2. Click the latest run with a green tick.
3. Scroll to **Artifacts** at the bottom and download **screenshots** (a zip file).
4. Inside: a PNG for each screen (`light-…` and `dark-…`) and `walkthrough.mp4`, a video of the
   whole walk-through.

To test on a real phone, see [TESTFLIGHT.md](TESTFLIGHT.md).
